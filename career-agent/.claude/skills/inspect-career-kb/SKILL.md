# Inspect Career KB

Report separately:
1. experiences and their confidence/status;
2. atomic evidence grouped by project;
3. reusable claims and their risk/qualifiers;
4. imported or unverified claims needing confirmation;
5. durable resume strategy rules;
6. contradictions, duplicated claims, and missing evidence-to-claim coverage.

Do not edit or commit. Prefer a small number of high-impact clarification questions.
