| Pretrained-model training at frontier scale | Missing | None | Do not claim |

## Positioning

The credible application is an evaluation-centered research engineer with strong human-AI behavior judgment and a demonstrated loop from qualitative failure questions to metrics, scenarios, evaluator systems, and model-development decisions. The main risk is the absence of direct LLM post-training and reinforcement-learning experience.
