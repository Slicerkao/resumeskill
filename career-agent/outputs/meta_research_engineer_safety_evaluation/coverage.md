# Meta Research Engineer, Safety Evaluation - Coverage

| Requirement | Coverage | Canonical support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLM, multimodal, and agent evaluation | Strong | Simulated users, agent-as-judge, LLM-as-judge, factual consistency, tool calls | No voice/video or multilingual evaluation evidence |
| Novel evaluation and benchmark design | Strong | Behavioral metrics, scenario generation, expert-calibrated gold sets | No established safety benchmark ownership |
| Measurement reliability and statistical rigor | Strong adjacent | Expert calibration, Bayesian fairness, experiment design, failure-rate analysis | No recorded power/saturation/contamination program |
| Reliable evaluation at scale | Strong adjacent | Existing internal batch platform, throughput/rate-limit debugging, versioning, dashboards | Did not build the distributed platform |
| Agentic risk and oversight | Partial | Tool-call behavior, sycophancy, visual memory, simulated-user stress testing | Not autonomy or jailbreak evaluation |
| Safety dataset creation | Adjacent | Expert gold sets, targeted scenarios, segmentation training-data curation | No adversarial or multilingual safety corpus |
| Anomaly diagnosis and reporting | Strong | Regression attribution, online validation, leadership model-release reports | Not live-training on-call support |
| Influence partner roadmaps | Strong | Systematic failures changed subsequent development direction | No sole roadmap or launch authority |
| Peer-reviewed evaluation publication | Strong | Listed author and experiment contributor, ECCV 2026 GH-ESD | Computer-vision evaluation, not AI-safety venue |
| Adversarial red-teaming and jailbreaks | Missing | None | Do not claim |
| Policy/regulatory translation | Missing | None | Do not claim |
| Large-scale distributed training | Missing | None | Do not claim |

## Positioning

The credible case is a research engineer specializing in measurement quality, multimodal and agent evaluation, human calibration, failure discovery, and decision-ready reporting. The largest risks are Meta-scale infrastructure ownership, adversarial safety depth, policy translation, and live-training experience.
